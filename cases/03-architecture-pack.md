# The Architect’s Covenant

*VALOR_Architecture_Pack · Authority, contracts and schemas*

**Status:** discontinued experiment. This account concerns the preserved 6 October 2026 snapshot.

## Intended problem

Separate advisory planning and orchestration from deterministic work-package state, document production and reporting.

## What existed

Architecture documents, contracts, schemas, libraries, test vectors and integrity tooling describe subsystem responsibilities and governed interfaces. An exact captured architecture commit is also the gitlink dependency of valor-build.

## Limits and documented issues

This is evidence of intended architecture and controlled artifacts. It does not establish a complete runtime, broad acceptance or a regulated release. The preserved README places the pack in a controlled pre-freeze, product-testing/field-trial posture.

The consumer build strategy describes the same pinned pack as frozen. Both statements are retained as a documentation inconsistency; this history does not decide that a valid freeze occurred. The original blueprint image and source standards material are excluded.

## Why this track ended

No specific decision ending this track is documented in the selected evidence. Detailed contracts and a stable pin are not substitutes for that missing decision.

## What to retain

The original [authority-boundary exhibit](../exhibits/01-authority-boundaries.md), which interprets the design without copying the blueprint. Keep the architecture identity separate from claims made by its consumers.
