# The Forge of Many Parts

*BUILD_VALOR · A modular factory and bounded acceptance*

**Status:** discontinued experiment. This account concerns the preserved 6 October 2026 snapshot.

## Intended problem

Preserve model authority while making a document factory more modular and inspectable.

## What existed

A factory package with module-owned builders, a shared model resolver, compatibility/tooling controls and a separate governance audit. Its preserved audit reports acceptance of a behavior-preserving refactor. The navigation compass explicitly supplies orientation rather than acceptance authority.

## Limits and documented issues

Acceptance of the refactor is bounded to its stated scope and recorded validation baseline. It does not establish completion of the wider model cascade, the later work-package planning slice or a finished product. The curation did not rerun the audit or source tests, and cited external acceptance evidence was not independently recovered here.

The source separates model-only integrity scope, identity from product state, and navigation from governing decisions. Stores, backups and some evidence are intentionally outside Git; repository restoration alone cannot recover them.

## Why this track ended

No specific stopping reason is documented in the selected evidence. Deferred scope remains deferred; a successful component refactor should not be erased merely because the wider experiment was discontinued.

## What to retain

The lesson that bounded acceptance can remain valid historical evidence without becoming a whole-system readiness claim. Original implementation, private operational evidence and governance payloads are excluded from this presentation.
