# The Reclaimed Chronicle

*VALOR_PROJECT_RECORD · Recovery and decision boundaries*

**Status:** discontinued experiment. This account concerns the preserved 6 October 2026 snapshot.

## Intended problem

Restore a trustworthy record of which artifacts were admitted, which decisions were accepted and what evidence remained missing.

## What existed

Governance/current-state documents and evidence-admission registers referencing implementation elsewhere rather than duplicating its code. Missing source evidence is recorded explicitly instead of being reconstructed from labels or memory.

## Limits and documented issues

The retained current-state document describes a proposed repair and keeps future routing frozen with substantive model-cascade completion unestablished. Separately, captured PR #4 metadata records a merge on 10 August 2026. The account preserves both the document wording and the later event.

Repository admission, evidence acceptance, operational completeness and authority to change a model are distinct. A merged repair or an accepted row/grouping decision does not establish the remaining substantive work.

## Why this track ended

The records show recovery limits and holds. They do not establish a repository-specific reason for discontinuation.

## What to retain

A sanitized explanation of those decision boundaries. Raw chats, inventories, registers, personnel information, original evidence files and local paths are excluded. The recovery record is a unique historical artifact even when it points to other repositories.
