# The Proving Ground

*VALOR_CQV_AGENT · A structured agent pack*

**Status:** discontinued experiment. This account concerns the preserved 6 October 2026 snapshot.

## Intended problem

Turn consultation and work-package behavior into a governed collection of rules, schemas, knowledge and deployment material.

## What existed

A Custom GPT pack rather than a conventional application. The README describes instruction, system, library, template, contract and deployment surfaces, with structural/policy validation and manifest tools.

## Limits and documented issues

The checks described concern required files, YAML/JSON structure, references, selected policy fragments and file integrity. They cannot by themselves establish correct generative behavior or human acceptance.

Claims of deterministic behavior, deployment readiness and site compliance are treated as historical claims. Site details, examples, standards text, pack files and validator code are excluded. The source declares GPL-3.0; that status is recorded without applying a new blanket license.

## Why this track ended

The selected records do not document a repository-specific stopping decision. Overlap with architecture and build work is evidence of parallel tracks, not proof that this pack was superseded on a precise date.

## What to retain

An explicit separation between configuration checks and behavioral evidence. The history should show what a validator can inspect and what remains outside its scope.
