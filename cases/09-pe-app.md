# The Unfinished Trial

*VALOR_PE_APP · Accepted M0 and an unmerged later proposal*

**Status:** discontinued experiment. This account concerns the preserved 6 October 2026 snapshot.

## Intended problem

Explore a narrower, server-controlled process-equipment workflow instead of inheriting an entire earlier engine.

## What existed

A synthetic tablet-compression-machine slice with Proposal commands, immutable revisions, idempotency receipts, SQLite state and audit records. The retained M0 record states acceptance of a synthetic checkpoint on 15 August 2026. AI support is a fake, offline, nonauthoritative substrate.

## Limits and documented issues

The source provenance record describes a clean foundation informed by BUILD_VALOR patterns and says implementation files were not copied. A limited snapshot comparison found no identical nonempty implementation/test blobs between their main tips; it cannot establish complete historical originality.

Later PR #5 closed unmerged on 4 October 2026. Its eight branch-only commits are preserved separately from main. Three unresolved reviews concern nested-row audit coverage, save-key collisions and stale test counts. P04 owner browser acceptance was not performed; earlier M0 acceptance and passing CI do not supply it. Broader lifecycle, real use and external-provider AI remain outside the admitted slice.

## Why this track ended

The closed unmerged branch is a documented endpoint for that proposed change. It is not, by itself, a documented root cause for ending the entire experiment.

## What to retain

A clear main-versus-proposal boundary and acceptance tied to exact scope. The private project declares Proprietary; source code, screenshots, raw records and external baseline/design material are excluded.
