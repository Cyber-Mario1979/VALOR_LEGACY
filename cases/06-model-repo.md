# The Guiding Star

*valor-model-repo · Governed content and identity*

**Status:** discontinued experiment. This account concerns the preserved 6 October 2026 snapshot.

## Intended problem

Make a selection/content model authoritative upstream of document generation, with explicit identity and admission controls.

## What existed

A governed current model, a factory/identity engine, tooling and change/audit records. Its current-model files match the corresponding BUILD_VALOR files at the captured main tips, while the full repositories differ.

## Limits and documented issues

The audit disposition describes gaps between checks applied to changes and checks needed for existing state, including duplicate identities and dangling references. Its schema-freeze criterion was corrected to require a second reporting consumer rather than completion of one equipment slice.

A separate orphan-mint disposition records identity allocation before refusal guards, a correction to guard-before-mint ordering and CLOSED/DISCHARGED status. It reports a clean tracked ledger and unaffected rendered documents; it does not report corruption of accepted outputs. Reader-side consistency checks cover only part of the problem.

## Why this track ended

These are documented bounded defects and corrections. They are not a documented decision explaining why the entire experiment ended.

## What to retain

The original [guard-order exhibit](../exhibits/02-guard-before-mint.md). Retain unique governance and defect records even where current model bytes overlap. Private model data, source code and raw session material are excluded from this presentation.
