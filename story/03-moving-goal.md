# The goal moved more than once

ASBP's dated records resist a single tidy destination. The approved May 25 redirect paused SaaS/productization around an incomplete CQV core. The June 2 change-control record still says DRAFT_FOR_REVIEW. Later v7 rules demanded explicit deliverables and guarded against completion through documentation alone.

June 9 records distinguish a conditional closeout from a governance-only continuation that was closed unmerged. On June 15, the README deprecated ASBP for scope drift, while older active-development wording remained below it.

**Documented fact:** these are differently scoped decisions and states. **Interpretation:** intent changed while artifacts retained earlier positions. The personal reasons for those changes belong to **Owner recollection**, not invented dialogue.
