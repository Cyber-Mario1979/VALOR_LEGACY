# What remained useful

Several useful ideas survive without a claim of inevitable improvement: explicit state, refusal before allocation, evidence with a known identity, and acceptance attached to a particular scope.

The owner's account of drift supplies collection context. It does not establish a stopping cause for every repository. Dated decisions, defects and unresolved reviews should retain their individual meanings.

The ten case accounts support this story. The original diagrams explain selected ideas and the case narratives retain their context and limits. This presentation includes no source-code excerpts.

**Interpretation:** an honest account can show useful work and real limits together.
