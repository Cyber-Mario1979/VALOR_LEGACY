# Giving the assistant a workbench

The earliest preserved repository anchor is August 2025. Valor-CQV framed advice through commands and work-package ideas; V7 later gave those ideas a layered pack with modes, templates and evaluation scenarios.

An instruction can make a request easier to express. Its existence does not establish that every conversation follows it. The preserved evaluation and integrity material asks a narrower question: what was packaged, and what could be checked?

**Documented fact:** these repositories contain instruction and evaluation artifacts. **Interpretation:** the workbench is a useful way to explain that packaging. The records reviewed here do not establish an earlier personal beginning.
