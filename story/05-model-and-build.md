# Making the model govern the build

The model and modular factory tracks make identity and mutation ordering part of the story. A recorded orphan-mint defect led to a guard-before-mint correction. Its disposition is CLOSED/DISCHARGED; the lesson must retain that closure rather than retell the defect as unresolved corruption.

The factory refactor has bounded acceptance for modularization and behavioral preservation. That conclusion does not accept every deferred feature. All 67 captured current-model files match between the model and factory snapshots, while their complete trees and unique audit records differ.

**Documented fact:** the recorded correction, scoped acceptance and snapshot comparison support those distinctions. **Interpretation:** a refusal should be judged by its state effects as well as its message.
